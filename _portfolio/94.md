I was glad to participate in the **CEDAR Next Generation EDA Workshop** on **2026-10-26** and give a talk titled **Beyond Geometry: Engineering-Aware Design Automation for Advanced Packages**, where I shared my vision and work in this area.

CEDAR, the **Centre for Next Generation EDA Research in Hong Kong** ([cedar.cse.cuhk.edu.hk](https://cedar.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/)), is a research centre led by CUHK, which is ranked World No. 1 in EDA by CSRankings. It is dedicated to advancing electronic design automation through AI, large circuit models, and high-performance computing, with a HK$75 million five-year research initiative.

In my talk, I discuss how to bring **industrial requirements** into the **design stage** and how to build **automated packaging design workflows** for advanced packages. The goal is to move beyond purely geometric design and make design automation **engineering-aware**, so that practical manufacturing and performance constraints can be considered early in the workflow.

Many thanks to the organizers for the invitation and to the audience for the engaging discussions!
