I was glad to participate in the **CEDAR Next Generation EDA Workshop** on **2026-10-26** and give a talk titled **Beyond Geometry: Engineering-Aware Design Automation for Advanced Packages**, where I shared my vision and work in this area.

In this talk, I discuss how to bring **industrial requirements** into the **design stage** and how to build **automated packaging design workflows** for advanced packages. The goal is to move beyond purely geometric design and make design automation **engineering-aware**, so that practical manufacturing and performance constraints can be considered early in the workflow.

Many thanks to the organizers for the invitation and to the audience for the engaging discussions!
