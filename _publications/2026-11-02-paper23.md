Large language models (LLMs) can translate natural-language design intent into parameterized layouts, but their output frequently violates the syntactic and semantic constraints of a real layout description language, and errors in the initial generation are rarely corrected. Existing retry loops are ad hoc, offer no guarantee-driven feedback, and often degrade the parts of a design that were already valid.

We propose SGLayout, a schema-guided closed-loop refinement framework for LLM-driven parameterized layout generation. SGLayout first constrains generation with a formal schema that encodes the grammar and legal parameter ranges of the target layout description, so that the model produces structurally valid output by construction. A closed-loop refinement stage then parses each candidate, uses deterministic validation to localize schema violations and unmet design objectives, and feeds structured, localized critiques back to the LLM for targeted repair, iterating until the design converges or a refinement budget is exhausted.

Experiments across parameterized layout generation tasks show that SGLayout substantially improves syntax validity, constraint satisfaction, and final design quality compared with direct LLM prompting and unguided retry strategies, while requiring no model fine-tuning.
